# Asset provenance

- **Generated by OpenAI image generation for this project:** `sky.png`, `sky.webp`, `elements.png`, `elements.webp`, and favicon derived from the elemental atlas. Original floating-world artwork and six-element icon sheet; no Spellbreak assets.
- **Kay Lousberg / KayKit, CC0:** Mage and dungeon ruin meshes. Source: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0 and https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Dungeon-Remastered-1.0 . License included at `public/assets/KayKit-LICENSE.txt`.
- **Quaternius Ultimate Monsters, CC0:** Demon (titan), Dragon_Evolved (wyrm), Yeti (colossus), Ghost_Skull (oracle). Source and CC0 declaration: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html . Files obtained from the official linked public download folder. Original glTF sources retained; optimized GLB exports ship at runtime.
- **Fonts:** Cormorant Garamond and Manrope through Google Fonts, with local system fallbacks. The fonts are the only optional remote runtime resource.
- **Audio:** Original Web Audio synthesis in `src/audio.ts`; no third-party recordings.

Terrain topology and arena assembly are original project code. Primitive rings and point sprites are used for spell VFX, danger telegraphs, and pickups, not character stand-ins.
